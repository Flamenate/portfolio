Hi, and welcome!

This website pretends to be a computer. Here's how to use it:

- Double-click anything on the desktop to open it. Yes, including this file.
- The Start menu (bottom left of the taskbar) introduces me at a glance, has quick actions for my résumé, and a few recommended files.
- The file explorer ("Haythem Frikha" on the desktop) has my documents, pictures, and a Recycle Bin I haven't emptied.
- Prefer typing? Open the Terminal and type `help`. Try `whoami`, `projects`, `experience`, or `fortune` if you're feeling lucky.
- The tray icons on the bottom right switch the theme and language.

Some things here aren't what they look like. Click around, there's more hidden than you'd think.

Nothing you type in Notepad is saved, so go ahead and write whatever you want.

— Haythem
